About SupaThrift

European Finds. Lagos Convenience. Better Prices.

Your trusted destination for European pre-owned clothing, gadgets, electronics and general accessories.

Welcome to SupaThrift

SupaThrift is a Lagos-headquartered retail and wholesale business focused on sourcing quality products through legitimate European supply channels and making them accessible to individuals, families, entrepreneurs and businesses across Nigeria.

We believe that great products should not always come with expensive price tags. Our goal is to connect Nigerian consumers with quality merchandise at affordable and competitive prices.

Who We Are

SupaThrift is built around a simple idea: making quality European goods more accessible to the Nigerian market.

We specialise in European-sourced pre-owned merchandise, alongside new clearance clothing, discounted retail stock and selected accessories.

Operating from our headquarters in Lagos, Nigeria, we combine international sourcing with local retail and wholesale distribution.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

European sourcing is central to the SupaThrift business model. We seek opportunities to acquire quality merchandise through legitimate European suppliers, clearance channels, surplus stock distributors and other authorised sourcing arrangements.

Our aim is to offer customers a diverse selection of products while maintaining transparency about product origin, condition and availability.

Products are clearly identified as new or pre-owned, allowing customers to make informed purchasing decisions.
